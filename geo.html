Search Is Changing. Is Your Brand Visible in AI Answers?

A growing number of buyers no longer scroll through ten blue links. They ask an AI assistant a question and trust the answer they get. If your brand is not part of that answer, you are invisible at the exact moment a customer is deciding.

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of making your brand, content and expertise easy for AI systems to find, understand, trust and cite. It builds on strong SEO, but goes further: clear entities, authoritative content, consistent brand signals and structured information that AI models can confidently use.

At Irshadify, we help you show up in AI-generated answers, not only in traditional search results.

GEO in Plain Language

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) means optimizing your brand and content so AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, Microsoft Copilot and Google AI Overviews mention, cite or recommend you in their answers.

Traditional SEO helps you rank in a list of links. GEO helps you appear inside the answer itself.

GEO does not replace SEO. A strong SEO foundation makes GEO work better, and we handle both together.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?

GEO is the practice of optimizing your brand and content so AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews can find, understand, trust and cite you in their answers.

What is the difference between SEO, GEO and AEO?

SEO helps you rank in search results. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) helps you win direct answers such as featured snippets and voice results. GEO helps your brand appear in answers generated by AI tools. The three work best together.

Can you guarantee that ChatGPT will mention my brand?

No. AI tools generate answers independently and results can change. No honest agency can guarantee mentions. What we do is improve every signal that increases your chances and measure progress transparently.

How long does GEO take to show results?

Some improvements, such as better content structure and schema, can influence visibility within weeks. Authority and mention building usually take 3 to 6 months or longer. Timelines depend on your industry and competition.

Do I need good SEO before GEO?

Strong SEO helps a lot, because many AI tools use search results and trusted web sources to find information. If your SEO is weak, we can fix both together.

Which AI tools do you optimize for?

We focus on the ones your audience uses most, typically ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Gemini, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot and Claude.

How do you measure GEO success?

We track a fixed set of buyer-style prompts over time and measure how often your brand is mentioned, how it is described, whether you are cited with a link, and any traffic or leads coming from AI tools.

Is GEO safe? Does it break any rules?

Yes, when done properly. We use only white-hat methods: helpful content, clear structure, honest brand signals and ethical outreach. We never use hidden text, fake reviews or manipulation.

Can GEO help my local or international business?

Yes. We tailor prompts, content and brand signals to the countries and languages you want to target.
