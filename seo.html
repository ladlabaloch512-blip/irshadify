Our SEO Services, Explained

Keyword Research and Search Intent Mapping

Every strong SEO campaign starts with knowing exactly what your customers search for. We research the keywords that bring real buyers, not just high-volume vanity terms.

What you get:

  • Full keyword research report (primary, secondary and long-tail keywords)
  • Search intent classification (informational, commercial, transactional)
  • Keyword difficulty and traffic opportunity analysis
  • Keyword-to-page mapping so every page has a clear target
  • Topic clusters to build authority across your niche

On-Page SEO Optimization

On-page SEO tells search engines exactly what each page is about and makes it a better experience for visitors.

What you get:

  • Optimized title tags and meta descriptions written for clicks
  • Proper heading structure (H1, H2, H3)
  • URL structure and slug improvements
  • Image optimization (compression, alt text, file names)
  • Internal linking strategy to pass authority to key pages
  • Schema markup (Organization, Service, FAQ, Breadcrumb, Article where relevant)
  • Content optimization using semantic and related terms

SEO Content Writing

Content is what ranks, earns links and convinces visitors to act. Our writers produce original, helpful content shaped around search intent and your brand voice.

What you get:

  • Service and landing page copy that ranks and converts
  • Blog articles and pillar guides
  • Content briefs built from competitor and SERP analysis
  • Content refreshes for older pages that lost rankings
  • Content that is clear, original and written for people first

Local SEO

If you serve customers in a specific city or region, local SEO puts your business on the map, literally.

What you get:

  • Google Business Profile setup and optimization
  • Local keyword targeting and location pages
  • NAP consistency (name, address, phone) across directories
  • Local citation building
  • Review strategy and reputation guidance
  • Local schema markup

SEO Audit (Technical and On-Page)

Before we fix anything, we find out what is holding your website back.

What you get:

  • Crawlability and indexation check
  • Site speed and Core Web Vitals review
  • Mobile usability check
  • Broken links, redirects and duplicate content issues
  • XML sitemap and robots.txt review
  • Site architecture and internal linking review
  • Prioritized action plan: what to fix first, and why

Competitor Analysis

You do not need to guess what works in your industry. Your competitors already show you.

What you get:

  • Identification of your true search competitors
  • Their top-ranking pages and keywords
  • Content gaps: topics they rank for that you do not
  • Backlink gap analysis: sites linking to them but not to you
  • Opportunities to outrank them, ranked by effort and impact

Ethical Link Building and Digital PR

Backlinks remain one of the strongest ranking signals, but only when they are earned the right way.

What you get:

  • Guest posting on relevant, quality websites
  • Outreach-based link acquisition
  • Digital PR and mention building
  • Link reclamation (turning unlinked brand mentions into links)
  • Backlink profile review and risk assessment
  • No private blog networks, no paid link schemes, no spam

Reporting and Performance Tracking

You always know what we did and what it produced.

What you get:

  • Monthly report in plain language, not jargon
  • Tracking of rankings, organic traffic, leads and conversions
  • Google Search Console and Google Analytics 4 setup and monitoring
  • Monthly review call to discuss results and next steps

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does SEO take to show results?

Most websites begin to see early improvements in 2 to 3 months, with stronger results between 4 and 6 months. The timeline depends on your competition, website age, current condition and the consistency of the work.

Can you guarantee first-page rankings?

No ethical SEO agency can guarantee specific rankings because search engines control the results. What we guarantee is a transparent, white-hat strategy, consistent execution and honest reporting.

What is the difference between SEO and paid ads?

Paid ads bring traffic only while you pay. SEO builds long-term visibility that keeps delivering traffic after the work is done. Many businesses use both, but SEO usually gives a lower cost per lead over time.

Do you offer SEO for international markets?

Yes. We research keywords and competitors in the specific country and language you want to target and optimize your site accordingly.

Do I need to have a website already?

Not necessarily. If you have a website, we audit and improve it. If you are building a new one, we can guide the SEO structure from the start so you avoid expensive fixes later.

What do you need from me to get started?

Access to your website, Google Search Console and Google Analytics (if available), a short overview of your business, and your main goals. We guide you through the rest.

How do you build backlinks?

We use ethical methods only: guest posts on relevant sites, outreach, digital PR and link reclamation. We never use link farms, private blog networks or paid link schemes.

Will I get reports?

Yes. You receive a clear monthly report and a review call to discuss progress and next steps.

Do you also write the content?

Yes. Our content team writes service pages, blog posts and guides optimized for both search engines and readers.
