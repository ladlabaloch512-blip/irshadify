A Team Built Around Specialists

Great search results come from several skills working together: technical SEO, strong content, quality links and a well-built website. Very few people are excellent at all of them.

That is why Irshadify is built as a partnership. Each service is handled by a specialist who does that work every day, while our founder, Ali Raza, leads the strategy, manages the project and stays accountable to you.

The result is expert work without the overhead of a big agency, and one clear person to talk to.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who will actually work on my project?

Your project is led by Ali Raza or a named project manager, and delivered by the specialists matched to your needs, such as SEO, content or web development.

Will I have one point of contact?

Yes. You always have one person to talk to, so you never have to manage separate freelancers or agencies.

Is the team in-house or remote?

Irshadify works as a partnership of specialists, and our team is a mix: some members work together in-house, others work remotely from different cities. We choose the right specialists for each project instead of limiting ourselves to one location. You always deal with one project lead, and we agree on meeting times and communication hours that suit your time zone, so distance never slows your project down.

How do you make sure quality stays consistent?

Every deliverable follows our checklists and is reviewed by the project lead before it reaches you.

Do you work with agencies?

Yes. Our team also delivers white-label SEO, GEO and AEO for agencies. See the agency partnership page for details.
