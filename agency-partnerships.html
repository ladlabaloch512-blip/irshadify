Grow Your Agency Without Growing Your Payroll

Most agencies hit the same wall. Clients ask for SEO, and now for AI search visibility, but hiring, training and managing specialists is slow, expensive and risky. Say no, and you lose revenue. Say yes without the right team, and you risk your reputation.

Irshadify solves this. We work behind the scenes as your delivery team for SEO, GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) and AEO (Answer Engine Optimization). You sell and manage the relationship. We do the work and make you look good.

Whether you run a web design studio, a social media agency, a PPC shop or a growing full-service agency, our partnership model helps you add high-value recurring services with predictable quality.

Is a Partnership With Irshadify Right for You?

This program is built for:

  • Web design and development agencies whose clients need SEO after launch
  • Social media and PPC agencies that want to add organic search services
  • Marketing consultants and freelancers who sell strategy but need delivery support
  • Branding and PR agencies that want to extend into search and AI visibility
  • Growing full-service agencies that need extra capacity during busy periods

This may not be the right fit if you are looking for the cheapest possible service or for automated, low-quality link packages. We focus on quality and long-term results.

Choose the Partnership Model That Fits Your Agency

Model 1: White Label Partnership

You sell the service under your own brand. We deliver in the background.

  • Your clients never see the Irshadify name
  • Reports are branded with your logo and colors
  • You set your own pricing and keep your margin
  • We communicate only through you, or join calls as your "in-house team" when you ask

Best for: agencies that want full control of the client relationship and brand.

Model 2: Referral and Revenue-Share Partnership

You introduce clients, and we handle onboarding, delivery and reporting. Revenue is shared as agreed.

  • Lower workload for you
  • Clear, written revenue-share terms
  • Client relationship stays with you, or is shared, depending on the agreement
  • Transparent payment tracking

Best for: consultants and agencies that prefer to focus on selling and relationships.

Note: we will help you decide which model fits during your partner call.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does white label SEO mean?

White label SEO means we deliver SEO work for you, and you present it to your clients under your own brand. Your clients deal with you, while our team works in the background.

Will my clients know you exist?

Not unless you decide to tell them. We stay behind the scenes, and we are happy to sign an NDA. If you want us to join client calls, we can do that as part of your team.

Do you also offer GEO and AEO, or only SEO?

We offer all three: SEO, GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) and AEO (Answer Engine Optimization). This lets you offer a complete search and AI visibility service to your clients.

How does pricing work?

Partner pricing is set so you can add your own margin. You can choose fixed white label packages or a revenue-share arrangement. Request our partner rate card for current pricing.

Can I use my own branding on reports?

Yes. Reports can be prepared with your logo, colors and format, so they look like they come from your agency.

How do you handle communication?

You get a dedicated project manager as your single point of contact. We can work through email, Slack, WhatsApp or the project tool you prefer.

Do you guarantee rankings?

No ethical agency can guarantee rankings, snippets or AI mentions. What we guarantee is white-hat work, clear reporting and consistent delivery. We also help you set honest expectations with your clients.

What if I have only one or two clients right now?

That is a good place to start. Many partners begin with a small number of accounts and grow from there.

Can you take over a client who already has an SEO provider?

Yes. We start with an audit to understand the current state, then recommend a clean plan going forward.
