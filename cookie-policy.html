Cookie Policy

How this website uses cookies, and the control you have over them.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored on your device by a website. They help the site work, remember choices you make, and, only with permission, help the site owner understand how the site is used.

Essential cookies

These are needed for the website to function, for example security and load balancing, and remembering your cookie choice itself. They are always active and do not track you for marketing.

Analytics cookies

If you accept in the cookie banner, analytics cookies help us understand which pages are useful and how visitors move through the site, so we can improve it. They stay off until you accept, and you can decline without affecting your use of the site.

Marketing cookies

If advertising or remarketing tools are added to this website in future, their cookies will be listed here and will also stay off until you accept them. None are active by default.

How to control cookies

You can change your choice at any time by clearing this site's cookies in your browser, which will show the banner again, or by blocking cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may stop parts of the site working properly.

Changes to this policy

If the tools this website uses change, this policy will be updated and, where consent is legally required, asked for again.
