SEO Services

What is SEO and how does it help my business?

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is the process of improving your website so it ranks higher in search results like Google. Higher rankings bring more qualified visitors without paying for each click, which leads to more leads, sales and brand trust over time.

What does your SEO service include?

Our SEO service starts with a website audit, followed by keyword and competitor research, on-page optimization, content recommendations and regular reporting. The exact scope depends on your website and goals, and we agree it with you before we start.

How long does SEO take to show results?

Most websites see early movement in 3 to 4 months, with stronger results at 6 to 12 months. Timing depends on competition, site age, content quality and past issues. We share progress twice a month so you can see what is changing.

Do you guarantee first-page rankings?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee rankings, because search engines control them. What we guarantee is a clear strategy, quality work, transparent reporting and steady effort toward your goals.

GEO and AEO Services

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?

GEO is the practice of making your brand and content easy for AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews to find, understand and cite. It focuses on clear answers, strong brand signals and mentions on trusted websites.

What is AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)?

AEO means structuring your content so search engines and AI assistants can pull direct answers from it. This includes question-style headings, short answer-first paragraphs, FAQs and structured data, so your pages are chosen as the answer.

How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO aims to rank your pages in search results. GEO aims to get your brand mentioned or cited inside AI-generated answers. They overlap heavily, and good SEO is still the foundation, because AI answers often draw from sites that already perform well in search.

Can you guarantee my brand will appear in ChatGPT or Google AI answers?

No. AI systems decide what to cite, and no agency controls that. We improve your chances by strengthening your content, brand signals and third-party mentions, and we track how often your brand appears for buyer-style prompts.

How do you measure GEO and AEO results?

We build a list of buyer-style prompts, run them across tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Mode, and record whether your brand is mentioned and how you compare with competitors. We repeat this regularly to track change.

B2B White Label Services for Agencies

What is white label SEO?

White label SEO means we do the SEO work and you deliver it to your clients under your own brand. Your client sees your agency, while our team handles the research, optimization and reporting behind the scenes.

Who is white label SEO for?

It suits web design, marketing and development agencies that want to offer SEO, GEO and AEO without hiring an in-house team. It lets you add a service, keep your client relationships and scale without extra payroll.

Will you contact my clients directly?

No. We work only through you, and we keep your client relationships and project details confidential. You stay the single point of contact for your clients.

Can we sign an NDA?

Yes. If you or your client want one, we can sign an NDA before work begins.

Do you offer white label reporting?

Yes. We send reports as PDF twice a month, and you can pass them on to your clients. Contact partners@irshadify.com to discuss how you would like reports delivered.

How do I become an agency partner?

Email partners@irshadify.com with a short description of your agency and what you need. We discuss your requirements, share a quote and agree the scope before any work begins.
