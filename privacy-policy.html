Information we collect

We collect the information you choose to send us:

  • Enquiry details: your name, business email, company or website address, country, optional phone or WhatsApp number, optional time zone, the service and budget you select, your preferred timeline, and the description of your project.
  • Application details: if you send a careers expression of interest, your name, email, area of interest, portfolio or CV link and your message.
  • Technical information: standard server and security logs created when you visit this website.
  • Analytics information, only if you accept analytics cookies in the cookie banner. Until you accept, non-essential cookies are not used.

How we use your information

To review and respond to your enquiry, to prepare a proposal or quotation, to deliver services you engage us for, to assess expressions of interest for future roles, and to keep this website secure and working. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for third-party marketing.

Lawful bases

Depending on your jurisdiction, we rely on your consent, on steps taken at your request before entering a contract, on performance of a contract, and on our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries. You may withdraw consent at any time.

Cookies and third-party tools

This website uses essential cookies to function. Analytics and other non-essential tools are activated only with your consent; the categories are described in our Cookie Policy. Service providers we use to run this website and our email process data on our behalf under their own privacy terms. The specific tools will be listed here once the live setup is confirmed.

How long we keep information

Enquiry information is kept only as long as needed to respond, to prepare a proposal and, where work follows, to deliver and administer the engagement. Applicant information is kept only while relevant to possible future roles and is deleted promptly on request.

Your rights

You may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to object to how it is used. Contact us using the details above and we will respond within a reasonable period, normally within one month.

International visitors

Irshadify works with clients in many countries. Your information may be processed in the countries where we and our service providers operate, with appropriate safeguards where the law requires them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the business and the law develop. The current version will always be the one published on this page.
