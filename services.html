Generative Engine Optimisation

GEO

Strengthen content usefulness and discoverability in generative AI search experiences.

Buyers now ask AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews for recommendations, and those tools answer with a short list of businesses. GEO is the practice of making your brand the business those tools mention and cite. We structure your content and your wider web presence so AI systems can understand, trust and quote your business.

Answer Engine Optimisation

AEO

Structure clear, useful answers that search engines and answer platforms can understand.

Many searches now end with a direct answer on the results page itself: a featured snippet, a People Also Ask box, or a voice assistant reading one answer aloud. AEO is about owning those answers for your industry. We find the questions your customers ask and structure your content so search engines choose your page as the answer.
